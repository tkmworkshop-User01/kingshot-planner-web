Common questions

Can I use the planner offline?

Yes. The bundled calculators and local plans work offline. Sending feedback, opening an external website and Android advertising require a connection.

Does it change anything in the game?

No. KS Planner is an independent planning utility. It does not sign in to Kingshot, alter the game or automate gameplay.

Can I move plans between devices?

Automatic cloud sync and a dedicated plan-transfer feature are not currently available. Do not delete your existing installation expecting its plans to transfer to another device.

Why do Android and iPhone ads differ?

The current Android app integrates Google AdMob. The current iPhone and iPad build contains no advertising SDK. The privacy policy explains the difference.

How do I adjust a Fold's inside-screen layout?

On supported Android foldables, choose Settings → Unfolded layout → Small, Medium or Large. Medium is the default portrait shape. The outside screen keeps its normal width.

How do I request deletion of a support email?

Email tkm.workshop@gmail.com with enough context to find the message. See the privacy policy for retention and deletion information.
