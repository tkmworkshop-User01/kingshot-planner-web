Request data deletion

No planner account is required. This page explains how to delete local plans and request deletion of information sent to TKM Workshop.

Request deletion of support records

  1. Email tkm.workshop@gmail.com with the subject KS Planner data deletion request.
  2. Tell us which support conversation or information you want deleted. Include its approximate date or subject so we can find it. When possible, send the request from the address used for that conversation.
  3. We may ask for enough information to verify that the records belong to you. Do not send passwords, government identification or unrelated private information.
Email a deletion request

What is deleted or retained

For a verified request, we delete the requested personal information from support records we control, including applicable emails, sender/contact addresses, attachments, screenshots, player/kingdom context and diagnostic details.

We may retain information needed to meet legal obligations, prevent fraud or document the handling of a request. If an exception applies, we explain what is retained and why. Support history otherwise has no fixed retention period; it may be kept as long as needed for support and historical reference. This does not mean every support record must be kept after a deletion request.

Deletion from active records may not immediately remove provider-managed backup copies. Those copies follow the provider’s retention and deletion processes. We do not promise automatic deletion within 90 days.

Delete plans stored on your device

Delete characters or kingdoms inside the planner to remove those records. To remove all data from an Android installation, use Android Settings → Apps → KS Planner → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. This erases local plans and preferences.

On iPhone or iPad, deleting the app removes its local data; offloading can keep data. Device/cloud backups are managed separately in your device settings. Restoring a backup can restore older records. TKM Workshop cannot remotely erase local plans or device backups.

Android advertising and other providers

The Android edition uses Google AdMob. Requests to TKM Workshop cover records we control; they do not automatically delete records controlled independently by Google, your email provider or your backup provider. Use your Android advertising-ID controls and the providers’ privacy tools for those records.
