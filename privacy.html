Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026 · KS Planner by TKM Workshop

This policy covers the current iPhone/iPad, Android and browser editions. Core planning is local and works without an account. Platform differences are identified below.

Planner Data

Kingshot Planner stores planning information and app preferences locally on your device so the app can remember your characters, selections, targets, and settings.

Feedback

Diagnostic data is off by default and never included unless you choose to turn it on for a specific report. It resets to off again the next time you open the feedback screen.

Diagnostics include only the app, device, operating system, screen, theme, time zone, player name and kingdom context shown in the preview. Full character records and plans are not included automatically.

Only the listed diagnostic context is added when you opt in. Full character records and plans are not included automatically. Your email application handles the message you decide to send.

Photos and local data

No account or cloud sync is required. Device backups may include local planner data.

Choose only the photos you want to use. Images stay on your device unless you attach one to feedback and send it.

Delete a character or kingdom in the planner to remove it. Uninstalling removes local app data. Manage backups in your device settings.

Retention and deletion requests

Feedback emails and attachments may be retained as long as needed for support history and historical reference. There is no fixed retention period.

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email tkm.workshop@gmail.com.

Advertising & Third-Party Services

iPhone, iPad and browser: This edition has no advertising or analytics SDK and does not access an advertising identifier.

Android: The Android app uses Google AdMob. Google may process IP addresses, ad interactions, diagnostics and device identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.

Android: Google Privacy Policy

You choose whether to send feedback through your email or sharing app. TKM Workshop receives only what you choose to send.

Policy Updates

This policy may change as Kingshot Planner develops to reflect the features and third-party services included in the app.

How feedback reaches us

Feedback is not sent automatically. Review the message and attachments in your chosen mail or sharing app, then decide whether to send them. Your email service may include the sending address and its normal message metadata. Our support mailbox is hosted by Gmail. Mail and sharing providers handle information under their own policies.

TKM Workshop uses feedback to answer requests, investigate issues, maintain support history and keep historical references. We do not sell feedback or use it for advertising. Contact us to ask about or request deletion of personal information in support records. There is no fixed retention period; requests are assessed against applicable obligations and the records concerned.

Your choices

Steps to request deletion of your data →

Leave diagnostics off, omit screenshots, cancel an email draft, or use the planner without sending feedback. A diagnostic preview shows the report's device and character context before the composer opens.

Delete local characters or kingdoms inside the planner. Deleting the app removes the data from that installation; offloading an iOS app can retain its data. Restoring a device backup may restore older planner data. Backup and camera/photo permissions are managed in your device settings.
